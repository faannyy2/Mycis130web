Welcome to CCP

Community College of Philadelphia, also known as CCP, is a college located in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. CCP offers many different programs and courses for students.

Students can earn an associate degree, learn new skills, and prepare to transfer to a four-year college or university.

Why Choose CCP?

CCP provides students with different educational opportunities and programs. The college also offers resources to help students succeed in their education.

Assignment Reflection

  1. The most significant layout issue I discovered was that the content became too wide on larger screens and crowded on smaller screens. Some text and images did not fit properly, which made the page difficult to read and caused horizontal scrolling.
  2. To fix this, I used CSS properties such as max-width, width: 100%, and flexible spacing. I also used Flexbox to organize the sections and added media queries to change the layout at different screen sizes.
  3. At narrow viewport widths, the columns changed into a single vertical layout. Images became smaller, and the text adjusted to fit the screen. At wide viewport widths, the content appeared in multiple columns with more space between sections.
  4. Testing the page helped me understand that a design can look correct on one screen but still have problems on another. I learned that checking different viewport widths is important for creating a responsive website. I also noticed that small changes in padding, font size, and image width can greatly improve readability.
  5. The next improvement I would make is adding a mobile navigation menu and testing the page on more real devices.
